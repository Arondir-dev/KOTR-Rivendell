The High Laws of Rivendell

Law of Sanctuary Rivendell is a place of refuge. No violence, pursuit, or hostile intent may be carried into the Valley. Judgment: Removal from Rivendell or direct judgment by Lord Elrond.
Law of Lore All knowledge shared must be truthful and respectful of the ancient records. Forbidden or sealed lore may not be accessed without permission. Judgment: Restricted library access or service within the archives.
Law of Tranquility The peace of Imladris shall not be broken by needless quarrel, disorder, or reckless noise. Judgment: Temporary removal from gatherings or halls.
Law of Healing No corrupted, shadowed, or harmful arts may be practiced within the House of Elrond. Judgment: Cleansing rites or banishment from the Halls of Healing.
Law of Respect All who dwell or seek shelter here—elves, guests, loremasters, and healers—shall be treated with equal honor. Judgment: Public apology or loss of privileges.
Law of Peaceful Arms Weapons must remain peace-tied and unused within the Valley of Rivendell. Judgment: Confiscation or formal reprimand.
Law of the Hidden Paths The secret ways into Rivendell are not to be revealed without leave from Lord Elrond. Judgment: Oath of silence or removal from strategic councils.
Law of Truth No deceit, false testimony, or manipulation shall take root in the Last Homely House. Judgment: Correction of records or service to the Loremasters.
Law of Conduct Feasts, councils, and halls must remain places of dignity, respect, and restraint. Judgment: Removal from events or assignment to humble service.
Law of Elrond’s Word The word and judgment of Lord Elrond are final and binding. Judgment: As decreed by the Lord of Imladris.

"May the peace of Rivendell endure within these halls, and within all who walk them."
